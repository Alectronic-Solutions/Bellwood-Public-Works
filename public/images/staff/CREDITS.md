# Staff portrait credits

The people in these portraits are photographic models, not the fictional employees
named beside them on the site. Bellwood Public Works is a demonstration project and
none of the staff records are real.

All twelve portraits are from Pexels and used under the Pexels License, which permits
free use including commercial use and does not require attribution. Attribution is
recorded here anyway so the source of every asset in this repository is traceable.
